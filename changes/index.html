2. Data reconciliation (worth knowing before the customer asks)

The raw extract overstates demand. The Site Data tab sums to 95,280 tickets/yr, but ten sites appear twice with rounded and unrounded values (Mississauga, Montreal, Santiago, Mumbai HO, Paris-Alto, Cergy, Toulouse, Casablanca, Londrina, Campo Novo). Removing the duplicates gives 84,750 observed tickets/yr, essentially flat against v1's 84,884. True demand did not grow 12%; it did not grow at all.

  • Join across the two tabs: 846 sites matched (the addresses tab suffixes every name with "-SDWAN"); 60 demand-carrying sites have no address record; 83 address records have no ticket history (new sites).
  • New sites carry estimated demand. The 83 new sites (1,429 users) are sized at the estate rate of 1.68 tickets/user/yr = 2,402 tickets/yr, flagged "estimated" in the Site Roster until history exists.
  • Placeholder names. "NO Deal A Network" (India) and "No Firewall" (Philippines) each cover two distinct sites in different cities; split and suffixed by city in the roster.

4. The staffing ratio: 453:1, derived and defensible

At 114 field technicians against 51,680 users the model implies 1 technician per 453 users (remote tails included). It is an output of the volume-based sizing, standing on:

  • A) Call volume projections: 87,163 tickets/yr converted to daily rates per site
  • B) Distribution of sites: 989 sites in 104 countries; geography sets minimum teams regardless of seats
  • C) Estimated time on task: ~6 incidents + 4 requests on-site and ~5 remote per tech/day at 85% utilization
  • D) Data gaps handled conservatively: no per-site time-on-task or ticket-type data yet; the ratio firms up as it lands

Regional spread: NAM 234:1 . LATAM 290:1 . EMEA 423:1 . APJC 706:1. The spread is the model following the work.

Site-staffing ladder: 1 tech at every site = 989 techs (52:1); 75% staffed = 742 (70:1); 50% = 494 (105:1); 25% = 247 (209:1) with three quarters of sites unstaffed. The model: 114 (453:1) covering 100% of sites, the headcount equivalent of staffing 11.5% of the estate.

Road map: 90 techs at 750:1 (proposed long-term goal), 74 at 1000:1 (stretch horizon), 67 at 1200:1 and 63 at 1500:1 (art of the possible). Campus geographic minimums kept; each step unlocked by adoption data, stepped down by rule.

The trade: each rung shifts cost, shipping, depot throughput, smart-hands dispatch and OEM warranty rise (Dell OEM warranty = strongest version of moving break-fix onto the manufacturer), offset by remote-first resolution and consolidated stock control. End state: Intune Autopilot + Dell Life Cycle Hub, installs and refresh become shipping events.

China data quirk: 6,417 reported users but only ~5 tickets/day (0.20 tix/user/yr, one-eighth the estate rate). Worth validating with Deal A; it materially skews any pure ratio view.

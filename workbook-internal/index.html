User-Ratio Sizing INTERACTIVE

The demand model implies 453 users per technician (51,680 / 114, remote tails included). That derived ratio is the default; type any ratio and every campus recomputes live.
Users per technician: derived default 453:1, not assumed. Try 750, 1000, 1200, 1500.
The road map (campus geographic minimums kept; unlocked by adoption, re-derived by rule):

Why 453:1 is the justified day-one default: (A) call volume projections, 87,163 tickets/yr to daily rates per site; (B) distribution of sites, 989 in 104 countries, geography sets minimum teams; (C) estimated time on task, ~6 incidents + 4 requests on-site and ~5 remote per day at 85% utilization; (D) deliberately conservative allowances where per-site time-on-task and type classification are not yet available.

The trade behind the road map: each rung shifts cost, shipping, depot throughput, smart-hands dispatch and OEM warranty rise (Dell OEM warranty is the strongest expression of moving break-fix to the manufacturer), offset by remote-first resolution and consolidated stock control. End state: Intune Autopilot + Dell global Life Cycle Hub, installs and refresh become shipping events.

China quirk: 6,417 reported users but ~5 tickets/day (0.20 vs the 1.68 estate rate); it skews any pure ratio view.

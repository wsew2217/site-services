Input headers

Required mindset: site_id (or site_name), country, and at least one demand signal. Address fields improve geocoding. Optional latitude / longitude let you --skip-geocode.

FieldNotes
site_idStable key
site_nameDisplay name
address_full · city · state_province · postal_code · countryGeocode inputs
users · seat_countSizing helpers
tickets_per_day · tickets_per_month · tickets_per_yearPriority: day → month → year (252 WD / 12 mo)
region · district · customer_site_type · notesOptional context
latitude · longitudeOptional; starter pack includes them

Skill: Review and Override Guide

Override path (when time-boxed):
1. Record the change in 06_Overrides with who/when/why
2. Note the site_id and previous mapped_role
3. Update Notes / exception_flags so the next reviewer sees it
4. Do not pretend the mapper produced the override

Common override cases:
- Known bad geocode but known city centroid is acceptable for ROM
- Temporary Staffed designation pending address cleanup
- Dispatch partner exception for embargoed / customs-locked countries

Never override to hide missing demand — escalate instead.

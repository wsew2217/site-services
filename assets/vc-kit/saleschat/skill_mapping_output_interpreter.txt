Skill: Mapping Output Interpreter

Explain VC_Mapped_Sites and VC_Summary.

Roles:
- Staffed = campus / hub with on-site presence assumption
- Local = inside local range of a Staffed campus
- Remote = outside local range and below remote threshold (dispatch eligible when enabled)

Key columns:
- mapped_role, is_hub, hub_site_id, campus_id
- distance_to_hub_miles, est_drive_minutes, within_local_range
- catchment_tickets_per_day, dispatch_eligible, exception_flags

Review order:
1. Exceptions
2. VC_Summary role splits
3. Staffed hubs on VC_Mapped_Sites
4. Remote sites and geocode failures
5. Cost_Model_Load readiness

If results look wrong, check coordinates, ticket normalization, and whether a far high-volume site should be Staffed.
